# Code of Conduct

MathDraw Studio is maintained as a professional scientific and engineering project. Participants are expected to communicate with precision, patience, and respect.

Acceptable participation includes constructive technical criticism, reproducible evidence, clear attribution, and recognition of differing levels of experience. Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, deliberate misrepresentation of results, plagiarism, and disclosure of private information are not acceptable.

Project maintainers may edit or remove contributions that violate these standards and may restrict participation when necessary to protect the community and the integrity of the project.

Concerns should be reported privately to the repository owner through the contact method listed on the owner's GitHub profile. Reports will be reviewed confidentially and addressed in proportion to their severity.
